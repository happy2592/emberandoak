Our Kitchen Philosophy

Honoring the ancient flame with modern precision.

We avoid gas griddles and artificial flavoring. Every flame in our kitchen is fueled exclusively by seasoned American White Oak and orchard cherrywood.

Founded in 2021 by Chef Marco Rossi and Elena Laurent, Ember & Oak was born out of an eagerness to step back from overwrought tasting menus and return to what matters most: primal searing, deeply caramelized crusts, silky hand-rolled pasta, and vibrant produce harvested hours before dinner service.

We paired this artisan food philosophy with an effortless ordering flow. Guests can browse our kitchen selections from their table or on the go, construct their dining order, and pay directly at our front register without clumsy online card forms.
