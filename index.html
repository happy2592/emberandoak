How To Order & Pay At Counter

Zero credit card forms online. Enjoy a frictionless dining experience designed for ease.

01

Select Your Favorites

Browse our live menu above and tap "Add to Order" on any dish, adjust quantities as desired.

02

Specify Dine-In or Takeaway

In your order tray, select your service mode and enter your table number or name for pickup.

03

Generate Your Final Bill

Tap "Generate Counter Bill" to receive your official itemized receipt and unique Order ID.

04

Direct Counter Payment

Walk up to our front counter, show your Order ID on your screen, and pay via Cash, Card, or UPI!
